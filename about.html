About Jaese & Jo Pharma Ltd

Pioneering ophthalmic excellence since 2015

Our Story

Jaese & Jo Pharma Ltd emerged from a shared vision: to provide Africa and the world with world-class ophthalmic pharmaceuticals that combine therapeutic innovation with patient comfort. With backgrounds in clinical ophthalmology and pharmaceutical chemistry, our founders recognized a gap in accessible, high-quality eye care formulations.


Starting from a modest research facility in Lagos, we have grown into a trusted name across 15+ countries, serving millions of patients. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing plant, inaugurated in 2020, operates under strict cGMP and WHO guidelines, ensuring every drop meets international quality standards.


Today, Jaese & Jo Pharma Ltd stands as a beacon of pharmaceutical excellence in sub-Saharan Africa, with a growing portfolio of innovative ophthalmic solutions. We remain committed to our founding principle: "Dedicated to Better Vision" — not just as a slogan, but as a promise to every patient and physician who trusts our products.
